Presets

A preset keeps every setting and the logo, except the stations and the data source. Home Assistant can switch to one with /api/set?preset=Evening.

Stations

Send a ticker message

Home Assistant uses the same call: /api/message?text=Door%20opened&ttl=60 (ttl=0 keeps it until cleared, &wake=1 lights just the ticker of a switched-off display while the message runs). Several messages take turns on the ticker, each until its own time is up. Needs the status row and its ticker switched on.
